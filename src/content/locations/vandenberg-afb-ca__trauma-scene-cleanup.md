## Why Vandenberg AFB Properties Need a Different Approach

Vandenberg AFB is an active Space Force installation, which means a meaningful share of the housing stock is base-managed and subject to access control that a typical residential job doesn't involve. Getting a crew and a decon vehicle through gate security requires coordination ahead of time, proper ID, and sometimes an escort, and that process looks different than pulling into a driveway in a civilian neighborhood. Off-base, the area around Lompoc and the surrounding unincorporated county land includes older ranch-style homes and newer tract housing, both of which have their own quirks when it comes to subfloor materials and ventilation that affect how thoroughly a space needs to be decontaminated and deodorized.

## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process in Vandenberg AFB

We start with a conversation, not a truck pulling up unannounced. For base housing, that means confirming access logistics with whoever holds point, whether that's a family member, the base housing office, or property management, before a vehicle ever approaches the gate. Once on scene, our IICRC-trained technicians assess affected materials, remove and dispose of anything that can't be safely restored according to biohazard handling standards, and apply EPA-registered disinfectants to any surface that was exposed. Odor is treated as its own problem: porous materials like subflooring or drywall often need to come out entirely, because surface cleaning alone won't resolve what's absorbed underneath. We document every step with photos for insurance and, where relevant, for base housing records.

## Insurance and Base Housing Coordination

Trauma cleanup claims move differently depending on who's paying. For privately owned homes, we document the loss and work directly with most major insurance carriers so the burden isn't entirely on the family. For base housing, there may be a separate process involving the housing office or a facilities contractor, and we coordinate paperwork so both tracks, insurance and base administration, get what they need without the family having to manage two separate conversations during an already difficult time.
