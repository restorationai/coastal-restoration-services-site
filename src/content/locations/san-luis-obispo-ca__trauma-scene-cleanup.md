## Our Trauma Scene Cleanup Process

Work begins only after law enforcement, the coroner's office, or other authorities having jurisdiction have released the scene. Surfaces that can be saved are cleaned and disinfected using EPA-registered hospital-grade products; anything porous that cannot be fully decontaminated, such as carpet padding or compromised drywall, is removed. The goal throughout is to leave no visible trace of the work that was done.
